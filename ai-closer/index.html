Why cascaded, not speech-to-speech

S2S (OpenAI Realtime / Gemini Live) is one locked box. Cascaded wins where it matters for sales: the most human TTS on the market, full control over the script, and freedom to swap any stage as the market moves. Prompt caching keeps the knowledge base cheap; transcripts feed analytics.

agent/system-prompt.md

Persona, humanity rules (pauses, fillers, one question at a time), sales method, guardrails.

agent/knowledge-product.md

The only source of truth. The agent may not state a fact that isn't in this file.

agent/objections.md

Playbook: acknowledge → find the real reason → answer → re-close. In Sarah's voice.

agent/call-flow.md

Call skeleton — opener, discovery, pitch, close. A skeleton, not a rail.

Re-skinning for a new client = editing these four files + config.yaml. Zero code changes.

🚫TCPA · DNC · quiet hours

Every list is scrubbed against the National DNC registry before dialing. Calls only within 8 am – 9 pm local. An opt-out mid-call is executed instantly and logged.

🛡Hard guardrails

The agent may only state facts from the knowledge file: no invented discounts, no income guarantees, no fake scarcity. Every claim in every transcript is traceable to a line in knowledge-product.md.
